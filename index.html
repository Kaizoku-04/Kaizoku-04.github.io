Work

Autonomous sumo robot

HTU Sumo Robot Contest, 2024

A fully autonomous sumo robot on an ESP32: it finds the opponent with its sensors and drives it out of the ring, with no remote control. I built the chassis, the wiring and the detection and motion logic, and competed with it live at Al-Hussein Technical University.

To train the behaviour before testing on hardware, I rebuilt the robot and the ring in Unity and trained an agent with ML-Agents (PPO, about one million steps). Ray sensors are its only view of the world. It earns reward for pushing the box over the edge and is penalised for leaving the ring itself.

FloraBot

Autonomous agricultural robot, 2025 to 2026, supported by JODDB

A field robot that drives greenhouse rows, checks every plant and recommends how much water and nitrogen each one needs. Its vision pipeline detects leaves with YOLO11, cuts them out with Segment Anything and classifies disease with ResNet-50 (97.6% combined validation accuracy). Florence-2 writes a plain-language note for each plant, and a multispectral camera adds NDVI.

The decisions come from a Deep Q-Network trained in the DSSAT crop simulator. I tested navigation in a simulated greenhouse in Gazebo before running in the field, and everything reports to a Flutter dashboard. I also produced the exhibition videos for SOFEX 2026.

OpenAMR Swarm

Warehouse robot fleet, 2026

A decentralised fleet of autonomous mobile robots that runs a whole warehouse box flow: receiving, storage, outbound, charging and recovering from faults. Each robot plans its own route through a shared lane graph, docks with AprilTags and hands boxes to UR10e arm cells. The full floor runs in NVIDIA Isaac Sim 6 with ROS 2 Jazzy and Nav2.

I own the robot and physical layer: the Isaac Sim worlds, navigation, docking, the swarm agent and the arm cells. I also led the redesign of the robot itself, OpenAMR v2. The renders below show the new chassis.
